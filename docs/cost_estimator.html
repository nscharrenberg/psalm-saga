Settings

Prices and models

USD per million tokens on OpenRouter. These prices reproduce every recorded cost_usd of the measured runs. Untick a writer or judge to leave it out.

All four runs used reasoning effort medium, so only medium is measured. None drops the reasoning tokens and keeps the visible answer; that gives the cost, not the effect on quality. Low and high scale the measured reasoning by the factors below. The reasoning share is editable per model, for example to give Claude reasoning: a custom share keeps the measured answer tokens and adds reasoning until it makes up that share of the output. Clear the field or press reset to return to the measured tokens.

Design

Samples per item and policy setting. The paper's design runs the full procedure 3 times, every other setup once, and paraphrase only under preserve: 19 runs per item and writer.

Evaluation extras

modelled

Instruments of the judge: block that run once per experiment, not once per run. None of the measured runs included them; their size is derived from the measured judge calls.

How the setups differ from the full procedure

modelled

Only the full procedure was measured. Every other setup uses the full procedure's tokens per stage times these factors (input / output); 0 removes a stage. Judges rate every setup.

Range bands

judgement

How far each kind of cost may fall below or rise above its estimate. The high end adds the contingency on top, for retries and failed calls the page cannot price. Changing a band changes every option's range.

Corpus

The 29 items of experiments/data/stories/corpus.csv. Cost scales with the source's length in characters. Untick an item to leave it out. Cost per item is one full-procedure run per writer, before contingency.

Reference

Where the numbers come from
  • Measured. Tokens per stage from four runs of the full procedure at reasoning effort medium: the shortest item (De vliegende Robert, 1,720 characters, Dutch) and the longest (A Scandal in Bohemia, 46,377 characters, English), once per writer. Gemma's short run recorded $0 because no price was set; its tokens are priced at the rates of the other Gemma run.
  • Scaling. For each writer and stage, a straight line through the two runs, by source characters. Every corpus item lies between them. Length and language are confounded: the short runs are Dutch and the long runs English. Each point is a single run.
  • Modelled. The four other setups (factors under How the setups differ), the PSALM anchor (85% of a run's judge tokens per pair), the alignment anchor (priced as the writer's recover stage) and G2 (14% of a run's judge tokens per comparison).
  • Guessed. Output at an effort other than medium, custom reasoning shares, and items outside the measured length range.
  • Not priced. Failed calls and retries. The Mistral Dutch run had 12 failed draft retries in which reasoning used up the output allowance; they carry no usage. The contingency covers these.
  • Data version . Saved estimates record it; when it changes, they show their saved figures next to the current ones.
